bridge-overhang-test.stl - PrintCraft's own test part for bridges and overhangs.
Made for this project (no outside source); free to use like the rest of PrintCraft.

Size: 145 x 35 x 27.5 mm. Two closed pieces, each a side profile extruded 10 mm deep.
Print without supports, so the bridges and overhangs are tested as they are.

Front piece - bridges:
  six 5 mm pillars, 7 mm tall, under a 1 mm deck, leaving gaps of
  10, 15, 20, 30 and 40 mm (left to right). PrintCraft bridges the deck's
  first layer (z 7.2 mm at 0.2 mm layers) across every gap.

Back piece - overhangs:
  a 5 mm wall with four 5 mm tall wedges whose undersides lean
  30, 45, 60 and 70 degrees from vertical (bottom to top), then a flat
  10 mm shelf on top. The wedges and the shelf are held on one side only,
  so they are overhangs, not bridges.
